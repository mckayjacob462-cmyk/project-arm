About

63 G and M codes for the Hardinge VMC with Fanuc 0i‑18M control, taken from the two RIT shop sheets (RIT0292a‑Schwenzer, RIT0293a‑Schwenzer).

Card text follows the sheets. The plain-English notes, the cautions and the animations are study material written around them — a canned cycle or an option code can behave differently on another machine, and the control at the machine is the authority.
